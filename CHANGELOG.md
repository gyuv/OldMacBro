# Changelog

All OldMacBro releases. Download any version from the **[Releases page](https://github.com/gyuv/oldmacbro/releases)**.

From v1.4.0: an Intel and an Apple Silicon DMG. Up to v1.3.0: Intel (x86_64) only. All need macOS 12 or newer and are ad-hoc signed (on first launch, right-click **OldMacBro.app** and choose **Open**).

## v1.4.0 · 2026-10-06
- **New: Apple Silicon edition.** Native arm64 build for M1–M4 Macs with P-core/E-core readings, M-series sensors, fanless-aware Thermals, Rosetta detection in Smart Boost and an Intel-only apps filter in the Uninstaller.
- Every release now ships two DMGs (`-Intel` and `-AppleSilicon`) plus `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
